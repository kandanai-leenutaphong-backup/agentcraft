Incident details 
This report relates to a reviewable operating incident4 that occurred at 1334 hrs on 16 October 2025 in Victoria. The incident 
involved the trip of the Yallourn ‘W’ Power Station (YWPS) unit 3 and unit 4. Each unit trip involved the disconnection of its 
associated 220/20 kilovolt (kV) transformer. 
Incident 
classification 
Protection/control system – cooling water isolating valve actuator control circuit was damaged, likely due to long-term flooding 
in the valve pit, which allowed the inadvertent remote electrical operation of the valve while the valve actuator was in a local 
manual operation mode. 
Generation impact 
A total of 597 megawatts (MW) of generation was lost as a result of this incident. 
Customer load 
impact 
No load was lost as a result of this incident. 
Pre-incident 
conditions 
Prior to the incident, YWPS unit 3 was generating 299 MW and unit 4 was generating 298 MW. 
There were sufficient units online in Victoria for system strength with Loy Yang A (LYA) Power Station (PS) unit 1, LYA PS unit 3, 
Loy Yang B (LYB) PS unit 2, YWPS unit 1, YWPS unit 3 and YWPS unit 4 online. Dartmouth PS and Newport PS were bid 
unavailable, which reduced the available system strength combinations. 
EnergyAustralia was undertaking commissioning of a new alternating current (AC) distribution supply board to replace the 
existing distribution board which supplies the cooling system common to YWPS unit 3 and unit 4. The YWPS unit 3 and unit 4 
common cooling system is separate to that of YWPS unit 1 and unit 2. 
Incident key events  
On 16 October 2025 the following events occurred: 
1. At 1334.29 hrs, YWPS unit 4 tripped immediately following the electrical operation of the valve actuator. 
2. At 1334.32 hrs, YWPS unit 3 tripped (see Figure 1). 
3. At 1350 hrs, AEMO directed Jeeralang units A2, A3 and B3 to synchronise and follow targets to manage system strength. 
– Jeeralang unit A2 was synchronised to the network at 1401 hrs. 
– Jeeralang unit A3 was synchronised to the network at 1401 hrs. 
– Jeeralang unit B3 was synchronised to the network at 1405 hrs. 
4. At 1627 hrs, YWPS unit 3 was resynchronised to the network. 
5. At 1755 hrs, AEMO cancelled the directions to Jeeralang units A2, A3 and B3 from 1800 hrs on 16 October 2025. 
On 17 October 2025, at 0152 hrs, YWPS unit 4 was resynchronised to the network. 
Incident cause 
Post-incident investigation by EnergyAustralia has concluded that the trip of YWPS unit 3 and unit 4 was caused by a loss of 
cooling water flow to both units. This occurred during commissioning works to replace the cooling system power supply 
distribution board. A cooling water isolating valve actuator operated unexpectedly, resulting in the valve closing and 
interrupting cooling water supply common to both units.  
The isolating water valve actuator control circuit, likely damaged due to long-term flooding in the pit, allowed an inadvertent 
remote electrical operation from the new distribution board while the valve actuator was in a local manual operation mode 
(electrically isolated).
